Title: Workforce Management — Forecast/Schedule mismatch after Wave 2 update
Issue ID: KI-004
Severity: P2
Symptoms:

Forecast view shows updated demand, but schedules don’t regenerate

Staffing gap warnings remain even after “Recalculate Forecast”

Agents see outdated staffing requirements in intraday view
Root cause: Background WFM forecast job completes, but schedule regeneration job remains queued due to stuck orchestration lock in Dataverse.
Workaround:

Release the orchestration lock and re-trigger regeneration

Temporarily run manual “Generate schedules” for impacted queues
Status: Workaround Available
ETA Fix: 2026-03-25
Affected versions: 2025 Wave 2
