1. GETTING STARTED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Navigate to https://[your-org].crm.dynamics.com
Sign in with your organizational (Microsoft Entra ID) credentials.
You will land on the Customer Service workspace.

First-time setup:
- Ensure your notification settings allow browser notifications
- Check audio device settings for voice calls
- Verify your queue assignments with your supervisor

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. SETTING YOUR PRESENCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Your presence determines if you receive work items:
- Available     : Receive calls, chats, and other work items
- Busy          : Only receive priority items
- Do Not Disturb: No items assigned (use during training/meetings)
- Away          : Auto-set after idle timeout
- Offline       : Not receiving any work

To change: Click your avatar (top right) > Select status

IMPORTANT: If your presence gets stuck on "Busy" after a call:
1. Sign out and close all browser tabs
2. Wait 2 minutes for session cleanup
3. Sign back in and set Available
4. If still stuck, contact admin to reset via Omnichannel admin portal

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. HANDLING INCOMING CONVERSATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
When a work item is routed to you:
1. A notification toast appears (with customer name if identified)
2. Click "Accept" within 90 seconds (configurable)
3. If declined or timed out, item returns to queue

For VOICE calls:
- Accept opens the call panel with mute, hold, transfer controls
- Customer context panel loads automatically
- Real-time transcription appears (if enabled)

For CHAT/MESSAGING:
- Accept opens conversation panel
- Type in the message compose area
- Use quick replies for common responses
- Send attachments via paperclip icon

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. CUSTOMER CONTEXT PANEL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The right-side panel shows:
- Customer name, email, phone
- Recent cases (last 5)
- Previous conversations (last 10)
- Active entitlements and SLA
- Custom fields configured by admin

Click "View full record" to open in new tab.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. KNOWLEDGE SEARCH
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
During any conversation:
1. Click the Knowledge tab (book icon) in the side panel
2. Type search keywords or let Copilot auto-suggest
3. Click an article to expand inline
4. Use "Send Link" to share article URL with customer
5. Use "Copy Content" to paste into response

Articles are ranked by relevance using AI search.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. USING COPILOT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Copilot appears in the right panel during active conversations:

FEATURES:
- Auto-Suggestions: Copilot suggests responses based on conversation context
- Case Summary: Click "Summarize" to auto-generate case notes
- Knowledge Suggestions: AI recommends relevant articles
- Draft Response: Click "Draft" for Copilot to compose a reply

TIPS:
- Copilot needs at least 2 customer messages to generate suggestions
- Review and edit suggestions before sending
- Use thumbs up/down to improve Copilot quality
- If Copilot is slow, check Azure OpenAI quota with admin

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7. CASE MANAGEMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
To create a new case from conversation:
1. Click "Create Case" in the command bar
2. Fill in Title, Description, Priority
3. Link to current conversation (auto-linked if enabled)
4. Save

To update existing case:
1. Open customer context panel
2. Click the linked case
3. Update fields and save

Case statuses: Active > In Progress > Waiting > Resolved > Closed

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
8. MULTI-SESSION HANDLING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Enterprise license: Up to 6 concurrent sessions
Digital license: Up to 4 concurrent sessions

Sessions appear as tabs at the top. Click a tab to switch.
Active timers continue in background sessions.

Color coding:
- Green dot: Active conversation
- Yellow dot: Customer waiting
- Red dot: SLA breach approaching

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
9. TRANSFERRING CONVERSATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CONSULT (keep ownership):
1. Click "Consult" > Search agent/queue
2. Internal chat opens with the consulted agent
3. You remain the primary owner

TRANSFER (hand off):
1. Click "Transfer" > Search agent/queue
2. Cold Transfer: Immediate handoff (no intro)
3. Warm Transfer: Conference first, then hand off

NOTE: Known Issue KI-2025-001 — Voice warm transfers may drop the call
if agents are in different Azure regions. Use cold transfer as workaround.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
10. WRAPPING UP CONVERSATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
After customer disconnects:
1. Complete wrap-up activities (case notes, disposition code)
2. The wrap-up timer starts (default: 5 minutes)
3. Click "End" to close and become available for next item
4. If timer expires, session auto-closes

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
11. KEYBOARD SHORTCUTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Ctrl + Shift + A  : Accept incoming
Ctrl + M          : Mute/unmute (voice)
Ctrl + H          : Hold/resume (voice)
Ctrl + Shift + T  : Transfer
Ctrl + K          : Knowledge search
Ctrl + /          : Copilot panel toggle

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
12. TROUBLESHOOTING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Issue: No notifications appearing
Fix: Check browser notification permissions. Enable for *.crm.dynamics.com

Issue: Audio not working on voice calls
Fix: Check browser microphone permissions. Verify headset in system sound settings.

Issue: Copilot panel empty
Fix: Ensure feature is enabled by admin. Check you have the Copilot User role.

Issue: Customer context not loading
Fix: Clear browser cache. Check Dataverse connectivity status.

For more help: Contact your supervisor or open an internal IT ticket.
