this article? Role: Delivery Partner Admin and FTEs Organization: All Location:
All What is this article about? A Modern Identity (MID) account is a type of
Microsoft account separate from a corpnet account; used for support related
tools. This article provides details about receiving a Modern Identity (MID)
account password via a Microsoft Teams card. Table of contents 1. Overview 2.
Microsoft Teams card 3. Users are ready to receive their password 4. MID account
no longer needed 1. Overview The article provides details regarding the VISOps
team sending a Microsoft Teams card to inform users that their new Microsoft
Identity (MID) account and password are ready, or for a MID account password
reset. Receiving a password for a MID account requires a voice call between the
user and the VISOps team. The Teams card offers users the option to connect with
the VISOps team via a voice call. Using a Microsoft Teams card, sent to the
user’s corpnet account, the users can select Yes or No to receive the password
for their MID account. Important: Receiving a password for a MID account must
occur via a voice chat with the VISOps team. Return to top 2. Microsoft Teams
card Once the VISOps team creates the MID account or the user submits the
password reset request, the VISOps will send a Microsoft Teams card to the end
user via their Teams chat associated with their corpnet account
(@microsoft.com). The sender is Workflows as shown below: Figure one: Workflows
– Sent a card Return to top 3. Users are ready to receive their password The
user selects Yes in the Teams card message, when ready and available for their
password. The Teams card will go into a group Teams chat for all VISOps team
members. A VISOps team member will then initiate a Teams chat with the user and
call the user at a time that is convenient for them. If the user is on a call,
near the end of their shift, or on an off day when the Teams card arrives, they
simply leave the card in the chat and select Yes at another time when they are
available. The card will not expire for at least 30 days. Figure two: VISOps is
ready to share the password Notes: If the user selects Yes on the Teams card but
they do not receive a response from the VISOps team, or it occurs at a time when
they are no longer able to take the call then a new Teams card will be sent
within a day. If there is no response from the VISOps team within a day the
Delivery Partner Admin or FTE should open a new VISOps SNOW ticket. The VISOps
team sends out the Teams cards in waves to better load balance the volume for
VISOps. Return to top 4. MID account no longer needed If the user no longer
requires assistance from the VISOps team they can select No to decline any
further action on their MID account for the current request. The VISOps will
note that the user has declined MID account assistance. The VISOps team will
make no further contact on the request. Should the user need their MID account
password reset at a future date, the Delivery Partner Admin or FTE will open a
new VISOps SNOW ticket and start this process again to receive a new Teams card
at that time. Return to top Notes: First published: Apr 08, 2025 Keywords:
