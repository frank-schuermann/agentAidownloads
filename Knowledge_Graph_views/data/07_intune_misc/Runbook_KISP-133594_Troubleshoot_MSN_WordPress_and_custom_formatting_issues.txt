PROCEDURE:
================================================================================

If you're using WordPress on MSN or encountering formatting issues in your
articles, this page outlines common problems with block compatibility,
unsupported styles, and HTML rendering limitations. Why doesn’t my WordPress
layout appear the same on MSN? MSN uses its own article template, which
overrides WordPress styles. Layout blocks, theme widgets, and special formatting
may be removed or converted during ingestion. Which WordPress blocks are
supported? Common blocks like Headings, Paragraphs, Lists, Images, and Quotes
are supported. Quote styles and special formatting blocks like Verse or
Pullquote may lose styling. Why isn’t my audio embed working? Audio embeds from
WordPress are not currently supported on MSN. You may need to convert audio into
a video or include it on your original article page only. Can I use HTML to
style my article? Yes, but only in the body or slide image description field.
Disallowed tags will be removed, and deeply nested tags or inline styles may be
stripped. Why do some of my embeds fail? MSN only supports certain embeds like
Twitter, Facebook, and Instagram if they use HTTPS and follow the official embed
code. Unsupported third-party widgets may be displayed as plaintext. Why do my
line breaks or paragraph spacing disappear? Avoid using multiple tags or deeply
nested divs. MSN may clean up extra breaks or misaligned spacing that affects
readability.
