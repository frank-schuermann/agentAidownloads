Example of previous cases 1. Prerequisites Return to top 2. Scenario/Problem
statement Starting June 19, 2023, use this table
https://aka.ms/MCIBIEIneligibleReasons to help partners understand the reason
text that appears to them for each ineligible engagement they see in the
Associate Engagement step of adding/claiming customers. The reasons are ranked.
If multiple ineligible reasons apply, partner will only see the reason text with
the highest rank. Both Partner location ID and customer ID appear to be eligible
for desired engagement per the relevant BI reports linked in
https://aka.ms/MCIBIEPowerBIReportsLibrary but partner cannot create new claim
for customer. OR Both Partner location ID and customer ID appear to be eligible
in MCI Eligibility Report for desired engagement. Partner has already created
claim but when customer tries to use emailed link to give consent, they get
below error message in browser window: Once customer gives consent to one
partner on an engagement, any other partners will not be able to get consent for
that same engagement from that customer unless the consented claim is cancelled,
expired, or rejected unless the engagement allows customers to receive multiple
deliveries. You can confirm whether an engagement allows the same customer to be
associated to more than 1 active/approved claim by checking the "Max claims
allowed per customer" column in https://aka.ms/MCIEngagementsIDMasterList. In
this example shown in screenshot, AIM Assessment - Fixed Fee only allows 1
active/approved claim per customer ID, whereas AMM PL for ISV SAP RISE (L) has
no limit on how many active/approved claims are allowed per customer. If the
"Max claims allowed per customer" limit shown in
https://aka.ms/MCIEngagementsIDMasterList has been hit, do not share details of
claims from one partner to another - only advise that the customer already gave
consent and that we cannot divulge specifics per Microsoft's confidentiality
agreement with each partner and customer. Return to top 3. Investigation Search
in Partner Center (microsoft.com) and WorkshopClaimsAndPayments - Power BI
report for customer ID and engagement name to look for active or completed
claims that would prevent new claims from being made for that customer ID +
engagement. An engagement claim is considered Active or Completed if it is in
any of the following states: Draft/Customer Consent Received Submitted Under
Review Partner Action Required Approved Disputed Rejected (if rejection occurred
less than 30 days ago) If you cannot find matching engagement using same
engagement name, use Engagement ID to name mapping table here
https://aka.ms/MCIEngagementsIDMasterList to determine whether customer already
has an active or complete claim for matching engagement under a different name.
If you cannot find any active or completed engagement that would prevent new
claims from being made for this engagement + customer ID, return to #39691274
and proceed through the remaining steps in that scenario. If customer ID already
has an active or completed engagement and the "Max claims allowed per customer"
limit shown in https://aka.ms/MCIEngagementsIDMasterList has been hit, respond
as follows:Response for partner/requester: In addition to the qualifications
specified for each individual MCI engagement, customers who were already
nominated for an active or completed [ENGAGEMENT NAME] engagement will be
ineligible to receive that engagement again in MCI. Customer ID [ID] is not
currently eligible to [claim / to give consent] to a claim for [ENGAGEMENT NAME]
because they already have given consent on an active or completed claim for this
engagement with another partner. This customer will not be eligible to [be
claimed / give consent] for this engagement to any other partner unless the
current active claim is expired, rejected, or cancelled. Return to top 4.
