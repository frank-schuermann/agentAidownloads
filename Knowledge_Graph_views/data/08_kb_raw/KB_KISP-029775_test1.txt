team​​​​​​​ for more information. Plan 1 1.2 Online Reservation rules These are
the rules of eligibility for Online Reservation: 1. Program: Commercial
Enterprise (EA) and Enterprise Subscription only. 2. Products: Office 365,
Windows Intune, CRM Online. 3. Countries/Regions availability: All
countries/regions that currently support at least one of the following: O365
Intune CRM services There are three types of Stock Keeping Units or SKUs that
are configured for Online Reservations: Show details on SKU types Category
Standard Reservations: Customer can place reservations in VLSC MAC. Category
License Reservations: Customer can place reservations in VLSC MAC only if they
have a prior purchase or fixed pricing configured on the Enrollment for the
given product. All Active/Invoiced orders except reservations are considered as
prior purchase orders. Note: If the customer has a multiple tenant setup and the
order or fixed pricing was done on the lead enrollment, the ROC will configure
all billing enrollments as well. However, this will not work from bottom to top
- if for example the order was placed on a billing enrollment the changes will
not be made on the lead enrollment or on another billing enrollment. Example:
All Telco SKUs are configured with License Reservations due to the complexity of
these orders, as the service is not configured in all countries/regions.
Category not configured for Online Reservations: The customer accepts a formal
financial obligation to pay for all licenses they committed to (not just
licenses they used) during True-Up or the Annual Order process. There are also
SKUs which are not configured for online reservation at all. Examples: DOD/GCC,
BlackForest PFAMs. Microsoft Licensing Solution Partner needs to always place an
order. To check which SKU belongs to which scenario, escalate to Tier 3, they
have tools to check how the SKUs are configured. 4. The customer accepts a
formal financial obligation to pay for all licenses they committed to (not just
licenses they used) during True-Up or the Annual Order process. Plan 2 1.2
