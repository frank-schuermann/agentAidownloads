Customize your calendar view in Microsoft Teams for a tailored approach to
managing your schedule. In this article Change your calendar view Save a
calendar view Apply calendar filters Delete a calendar view View different time
scales Change your calendar view Your Teams calendar will display your work week
schedule by default, but you can choose to view your schedule for the entire
week or specific days only. Select your current view at the top of your calendar
to choose a different view. Apply calendar filters At the top of your calendar
or in More options , select Filter . Deselect specific types of events to remove
them from view. View different time scales Change your time scale in the new
calendar to adjust the amount of space for meeting details. To change your
calendar’s time scale: Select your calendar view (e.g., Work week, Week, etc.).
Select Time scale. Choose the time scale you want to view your calendar in. When
you choose a time scale, the change will be applied immediately. Tips: Increase
the time scale to see more of your schedule at once, but with fewer details.
Decrease the time scale to see your schedule close-up, with more details. Save a
calendar view When you’ve set the specific filters, calendar groups, and other
presets you want to see in your calendar, save the view to return to it later.
To save a calendar view: Select your calendar view. From the dropdown menu,
select Saved views > Save current view. Name the view and select Save. To apply
a saved view: Select your calendar view. From the dropdown menu, select Saved
views . Select the view you want to apply. Delete a calendar view To delete a
saved calendar view: Select your calendar view. From the dropdown menu, select
Saved views . Next to the view you want to delete, select > Delete . Related
topics Get started with the new calendar in Microsoft Teams View multiple
calendars in Microsoft Teams
