Applies ToMicrosoft Teams Microsoft Teams for Education Earn Reading 3-day,
5-day, and 7-day streaks to unlock valuable rewards in Reading Coach. This
feature aims to motivate students to engage in consistent reading habits.
Additionally, readers can monitor their progress over different time
frames—weekly, 30-days, and all-time—through an intuitive dashboard designed for
learners of all ages. This user-friendly interface not only tracks their reading
achievements but also encourages them to set and achieve new reading goals,
thereby fostering a lifelong love for reading. My Progress Achievements and
History, once on the Reading Coach homepage, are now under the My Progress tab.
Selecting this tab will load the following page: When you click on the My
Progress tab, you will see a new page that shows how well you are doing with
your reading. This page will have the following: Your profile photo as a student
Switches for three different time periods: reading week, last 30 days, and all
time. Choosing any one of the boxes for reading time, readings completed,
average accuracy, and correct words per minute will produce a graph showing your
progress. My achievements shows the badges you’ve earned over the selected time
period. And my stories displays stories you’ve completed throughout a select
period. This way, you can keep track of your progress and see how much you have
improved over time. Reading Streaks Reading every day is very important. It
helps you get better at reading, learn new words, and feel good about your
reading skills. When you read using Reading Coach, you can earn badges. If you
read for three, five, or seven days in a seven day period, you get special
badges called Reading Streak badges. You can check how well you are doing and
see your reading streaks on the My Progress tab. Reading Reward: This shows how
many minutes you need to read to earn a badge and keep your reading streak
going. Reading Streak: This shows the past seven days and keeps track of your
reading badges and how many days you have read in a row.
