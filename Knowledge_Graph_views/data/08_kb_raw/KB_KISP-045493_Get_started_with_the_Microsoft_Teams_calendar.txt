Streamline meeting and event scheduling, customize your calendar views, and much
more in Microsoft Teams for desktop. In this article Open your calendar Find the
right place to meet Change your calendar view Share your calendar View other
calendars Print your calendar Schedule and start meetings and events Quickly
access past meeting details Open your calendar To start using your Teams
calendar, select Calendar ​​​​​​​ on the left side of Teams. Change your
calendar view Your Teams calendar will display your work week by default. See
other views, like the entire week or month, by selecting Work week at the top of
your calendar and choosing one. You can also:​​​​​​​ Change the time scale to
adjust space for meeting details. Save calendar views to access specific layouts
later. Select Filter to customize the types of events that appear on your
calendar. Select Show navigation pane to see the mini-month view, view other
people’s calendars, create calendar groups, and more. Select More options >
Calendar settings to change your calendar appearance, adjust event and meeting
settings, and more. To learn more, see Customize your calendar in Microsoft
Teams. View other calendars View multiple calendars at the same time for
seamless coordination and event planning. Look at different people’s calendars,
calendars that highlight birthdays or holidays, and more. In the top-left
corner, select Show navigation pane . Under My calendars, create and select
calendar groups to quickly bring up views of specific calendars later. Switch
between split and combined views to see other calendars side-by-side or
integrated into one. To learn more, see View other calendars in Microsoft Teams.
Schedule and start meetings and events Easily schedule meetings and events of
all types from your Teams calendar. To schedule a new meeting or event: Select
Calendar in Teams. Select New or select a timeslot in your calendar. Select the
dropdown arrow next to New to choose from different types of event templates.
Select View all templates to see more. Select Scheduler to find a time that
works best for everyone. Enter meeting or event details. Select Save .
Important: When scheduling a virtual Teams meeting, turn on the Teams meeting
toggle. To join a meeting or event: Select Calendar in Teams. Select a meeting
or event. Select Join . To learn more about joining a Teams meeting, see Join a
meeting in Microsoft Teams. Find the right place to meet When choosing a room or
location for your meeting, you’ll see more details about available rooms, like
capacity and technical specs. You’ll also see the option to Browse with Places
Finder. To learn more, see Get started with Microsoft Places. Share your
calendar Share your calendar with others to give them insight into your schedule
and availability. To share your calendar with someone specific: Select Calendar
in Teams. Select More options > Share . Enter the email address or contact you
want to share with. Next to their name, select the dropdown menu to choose their
permissions. Select Share. Depending on policies set by your IT admin, you can
also manage permissions for other people in your organization in the dropdown
menu below. To learn more, see Share your calendar in Microsoft Teams. Print
your calendar Print out your calendar to distribute physical copies of it or
keep it on hand for markups and easy access. To learn more, see Share your
calendar in Microsoft Teams. Quickly access past meeting details Go right to a
past meeting’s recap, chat, and other details in your Teams calendar. To access:
Select Calendar in Teams. Select a past recorded meeting. Select Recap to view
the meeting recap. Select Chat ​​​​​​​ to open the meeting chat. To learn more
about recap in Teams, see Meeting recap in Microsoft Teams. Related topics
Customize your calendar in Microsoft Teams View multiple calendars in Microsoft
Teams
