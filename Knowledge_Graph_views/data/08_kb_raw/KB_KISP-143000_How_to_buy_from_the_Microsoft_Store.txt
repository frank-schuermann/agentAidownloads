The Microsoft Store offers deals on new or refurbished Surface, Xbox, and
accessories. You can also browse business deals and subscriptions and buy gift
cards. If you want games and apps, head over to https://apps.microsoft.com How
to complete a purchase from the Microsoft Store Visit the Microsoft Digital
Store webpage. Open your web browser and go to www.microsoft.com/store. You will
be redirected to the localized version of the site based on your location, but
you can change your country select at the bottom of the page. Choose the item
you want to purchase to view its details, including price, specifications, and
customer reviews. Scroll through the sale items or use Search at the top of the
page to find specific products on sale. Once you have added all the items you
want to purchase, select Cart at the top right corner of the page or select
Review and checkout to review your selections. Checkout. If you have a Microsoft
account, sign in by selecting the Sign In button at the top right corner of the
page. Tip: If you don't have a Microsoft account you can Create one, or check
out as a guest. Enter Payment Information. Choose your payment method and enter
the necessary details. You can use a credit card, debit card, PayPal, or other
available payment options. Learn how to Add a new bank or credit card to your
Microsoft account. Review and Confirm Purchase. Review your order details,
including the items, shipping information, and payment method. Select Place
order to complete your purchase.​​​​​​​ See also Add a new bank or credit card
to your Microsoft account Guest checkout Need more help? Contact Support For
pre-sales support, or issues with purchasing, contact Sales support.
