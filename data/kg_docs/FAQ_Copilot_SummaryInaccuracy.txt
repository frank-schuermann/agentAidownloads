FAQ: Why does Copilot sometimes generate incorrect summaries?

FAQ ID: FAQ-016
Q: Why does Copilot add follow-ups that weren’t discussed in the call?
A:
This can occur when the transcript provided to Copilot is incomplete or delayed.
When context is missing, the model may infer details, which can appear as hallucinations.
Wait for transcript finalization before generating a summary, or disable auto-summary until ingestion stabilizes.
