FAQ: Why am I redirected back to login after authenticating?

FAQ ID: FAQ-015
Q: I sign in via SSO successfully, but the platform sends me back to the login page. Why?
A:
This usually happens when the platform rejects the SAML assertion due to timestamp validation (clock skew).
The authentication gateway may treat the assertion as expired or not yet valid.
If this is impacting many users, apply the SSO clock drift recovery runbook and verify NTP sync.
