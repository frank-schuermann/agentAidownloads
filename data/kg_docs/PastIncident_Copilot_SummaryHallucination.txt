Incident: Copilot summary hallucination affecting coaching notes

Incident ID: INC-016
Severity: P2
Status: Resolved

Summary:
Supervisors reported inaccurate Copilot summaries showing fabricated tasks and incorrect outcomes.

Impact:
- Agents saved incorrect follow-ups in CRM notes.
- Coaching dashboards displayed unreliable “Next Steps”.

Resolution:
- Disabled auto-summary temporarily.
- Restarted transcript ingestion workers to clear backlog.
- Ensured summarization triggers only after transcript finalization.
