Incident: Mass SSO login failures during shift start

Incident ID: INC-015
Severity: P1
Status: Resolved

Summary:
A large enterprise customer reported widespread SSO login loops for agents at shift start.

Impact:
- Agents unable to access the CCaaS portal for ~18 minutes.
- Helpdesk ticket surge and delayed customer responses.

Resolution:
- Increased SAML skew tolerance temporarily.
- Rolling restart of auth gateway cluster.
- Verified NTP stability and reverted tolerance.
