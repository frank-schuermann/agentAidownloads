Runbook: Copilot Summary Hallucination Recovery

Runbook ID: RB-016
Category: AI Service Recovery
Estimated Time: 25 minutes

When to use:
- Copilot summaries contain fabricated details.
- Transcript ingestion is delayed or incomplete at summary time.

Steps:
1. Confirm issue:
   - Compare 3 recent transcripts to their generated summaries.
   - Identify hallucinated statements or tasks.
2. Check transcript ingestion:
   - Review ingestion latency metrics (p95) and completeness flag rates.
3. Validate trigger logic:
   - Ensure summarization starts only after transcript finalized event.
4. Apply mitigation:
   - Disable “Auto Summary” for impacted queues/tenants.
5. Restart ingestion workers:
   - Rolling restart transcript streaming workers if backlog exists.
6. Re-enable:
   - Once p95 latency stabilizes, re-enable auto-summary.
7. Validate:
   - Generate summaries for 5 recent interactions and confirm accuracy.
