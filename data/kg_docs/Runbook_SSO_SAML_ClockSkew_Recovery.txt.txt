Runbook: SSO SAML Clock Drift Recovery

Runbook ID: RB-015
Category: Authentication Recovery
Estimated Time: 20 minutes

When to use:
- Multiple users report SSO login loops.
- Auth gateway logs show repeated SAML assertion validation failures.

Steps:
1. Confirm symptoms:
   - Attempt login with a test user and capture the error.
2. Inspect auth gateway logs:
   - Look for “SAML_ASSERTION_EXPIRED” or “ASSERTION_NOT_YET_VALID”.
3. Validate time synchronization:
   - Verify NTP status on all auth gateway nodes.
   - Confirm drift is < 30 seconds.
4. Apply temporary mitigation:
   - Increase SAML clock-skew tolerance from 2 minutes → 5 minutes.
5. Restart auth gateway:
   - Rolling restart to avoid full outage.
6. Validate:
   - Test 3 logins (agent + supervisor + admin).
   - Confirm session token issuance and portal access.
7. Roll back mitigation:
   - Reduce skew tolerance back to normal once clocks are stable.
