Runbook: WFM Forecast → Schedule Regeneration Recovery (Lock / Queue Stuck)
Runbook ID: RB-004
Category: Incident Recovery
Estimated time: 25 minutes
Steps:

Confirm WFM schedule regen backlog in Admin Center → WFM → Jobs

Check Dataverse async jobs for “WFM Schedule Regen” status = Waiting / In Progress > 20 min

Inspect orchestration lock entity for stuck lock owner / stale timestamp

Validate schedules updated timestamp in WFM schedule table

Validate staffing gaps recalculated in intraday view

If still stuck: restart WFM orchestration worker and re-run regen

Communicate to supervisors + update incident notes
