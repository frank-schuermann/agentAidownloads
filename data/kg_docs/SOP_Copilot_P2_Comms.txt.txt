SOP: P2 Copilot Summary Accuracy — Supervisor/Agent Communications

SOP ID: SOP-016
Category: Communications

Goal:
Prevent incorrect customer follow-ups caused by inaccurate AI summaries.

Steps:
1. Notify supervisors:
   - “Copilot summaries may contain inaccurate or fabricated details.”
2. Agent guidance:
   - Manually verify summaries before saving notes or follow-up tasks.
3. Interim control:
   - Auto-summary may be temporarily disabled for impacted queues.
4. Update cadence:
   - Provide updates every 20 minutes until stable.
5. Close out:
   - Confirm transcript completeness checks are restored and summaries verified.
