SOP: P2 Quality Management Scoring Drift — Supervisor Communications

SOP ID: SOP-003
Category: Communications

Goal:
Keep supervisors informed while remediation is applied and prevent incorrect coaching actions.

Steps:
1. Confirm impacted queues/teams and time window (e.g., last 24–48 hours).
2. Notify supervisors:
   - Scores may be temporarily inconsistent due to a rubric sync issue.
   - Coaching actions should be paused for impacted items until re-score completes.
3. Apply the runbook and provide updates every 15 minutes until stable.
4. If drift persists > 60 minutes:
   - Escalate to platform on-call and open vendor/support ticket.
5. After recovery:
   - Confirm re-score completed.
   - Share summary: impact window, fix applied, and prevention action.
