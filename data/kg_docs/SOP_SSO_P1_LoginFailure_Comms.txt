SOP: P1 SSO Login Failures — Supervisor/Agent Communications

SOP ID: SOP-015
Category: Communications

Goal:
Reduce ticket spam, set expectations, and prevent repeated failed login attempts while remediation runs.

Steps:
1. Confirm scope:
   - Which regions/tenants are impacted and when it started.
2. Notify stakeholders:
   - “We’re investigating SSO login failures causing login loops/session invalid.”
3. Provide guidance:
   - Avoid repeated rapid retries (rate limit risk).
   - Retry after 10 minutes once mitigation is applied.
4. Updates:
   - Provide update every 15 minutes until stable.
5. Closure:
   - Confirm recovery and share impact window + mitigation applied.
