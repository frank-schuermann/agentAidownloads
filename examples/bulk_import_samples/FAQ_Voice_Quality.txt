FAQ — FAQ-Voice-Quality
Voice quality issues and troubleshooting

--- QUESTION ---
Why is the voice quality poor during calls (crackling, robotic, choppy audio)?

--- ANSWER ---
Poor voice quality in CCaaS calls can be caused by several factors:

**1. Network Latency**
- Check ping to Azure regions: should be <50ms
- Run: ping eastus2.azure.com
- If >100ms, contact network team

**2. Insufficient Bandwidth**
- Minimum required: 100 kbps per concurrent call
- Check current usage: bandwidth-monitor.company.com
- Consider QoS prioritization for voice traffic

**3. Codec Mismatch**
- Ensure G.711 codec is enabled (highest quality)
- Check codec configuration in voice gateway
- Avoid G.729 unless bandwidth is severely limited

**4. Packet Loss**
- Voice is very sensitive to packet loss (>1% is noticeable)
- Check QoS settings on network equipment
- Run: traceroute -q 1 eastus2.azure.com

**5. Jitter**
- Should be <30ms
- Check jitter buffer settings in voice gateway config
- Increase buffer size if jitter is high (trade-off: slight delay)

--- DETAILED TROUBLESHOOTING ---

For persistent voice quality issues:
1. Follow Runbook RB-Voice-Diagnostics for step-by-step diagnosis
2. Collect network traces: tcpdump -i any -w voice_trace.pcap port 5060
3. Check Known Issue KI-Voice-Transfer-Drop (may be related)
4. If affecting multiple customers, follow SOP-P2-Incident
