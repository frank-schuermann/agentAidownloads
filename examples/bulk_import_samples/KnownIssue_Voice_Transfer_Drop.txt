KNOWN ISSUE — KI-Voice-Transfer-Drop
Voice transfer calls drop unexpectedly

--- DESCRIPTION ---
ID: KI-Voice-Transfer-Drop
Title: Voice transfer calls drop unexpectedly
Category: Voice
Priority: P2
Status: Open

--- SYMPTOMS ---
- Call drops when agent attempts to transfer
- Customer hears silence then disconnect tone
- Affects approximately 5% of transfer attempts
- More frequent during peak hours (9-11 AM, 2-4 PM)
- Only affects warm transfers (not cold transfers)

--- ROOT CAUSE ---
Race condition in SIP session handoff during transfer.
The SIP REFER message times out before the target agent accepts the session.
This is caused by insufficient timeout settings in the voice gateway configuration.

--- IMPACT ---
- 50-100 dropped transfers per day
- Customer satisfaction impact: -2 CSAT points
- Requires agent to call back customer
- Average recovery time: 3 minutes per incident

--- WORKAROUND ---
1. Follow Runbook RB-Voice-Outage to restart voice gateway
2. Temporarily disable warm transfers in routing configuration
3. Use cold transfers instead (announce, then transfer)
4. Monitor transfer success rate via dashboard

For detailed troubleshooting, see FAQ-Voice-Quality.

--- PERMANENT FIX ---
Configuration update deployed in Release REL-2026-W12
- Increase SIP REFER timeout from 5s to 15s
- Enable session keepalive during transfer
- Add retry logic for failed transfers

- SIP Gateway (INFRA-SIPGateway)
